- `windowStart` auto-anchors to `totalItems - maxVisibleWindow`
- New `scrollUp`/`scrollDown` move windowStart freely; when user scrolls to bottom, `tailFollow` re-engages
- Existing `getVisibleItems` and `windowStart`/`windowEnd` work unchanged
